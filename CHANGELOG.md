# HiddenYatra — Changelog

All notable changes to the HiddenYatra project are documented in this file.

## [2.6.0] — 2026-08-21 (Milestone 34 Release: Virtual Exploration, Gastronomy & Eco-Immersion)

### Added
- **Virtual 360° Photo Spheres & Viewpoints**: 8K UHD interactive panorama viewer for Mahabodhi Temple, Nalanda Monastery, Vishwa Shanti Stupa, Rohtasgarh Fort, Barabar Caves, and Sher Shah Suri Tomb with pitch/yaw coordinate hotspots and historical audio commentary.
- **Bihar Culinary Heritage & Gastronomy Trails**: Comprehensive catalog of traditional dishes (Litti Chokha, GI-certified Silao Khaja, Gaya Tilkut, Maner Laddu, GI Mithila Makhana Kheer, Sattu Sherbet) with dietary classifications and verified heritage eatery locators.
- **Eco-Wildlife & Nature Reserves**: Detailed directories for Valmiki National Park & Tiger Reserve, Vikramshila Dolphin Sanctuary, Kanwar Lake Ramsar Wetland, Bhimbandh Thermal Springs, and Kaimur Wildlife Sanctuary with safari guidelines and endangered species tracking.
- **Rural Immersion & Cultural Volunteerism**: Community-based tourism residencies in Madhubani art guilds, organic Makhana harvesting cooperatives, and Nalanda youth docent programs with an automated skill-matching registration engine.
- **Universal Cross-Domain Discovery Search**: Real-time multi-entity search resolving queries across places, circuits, festivals, crafts, cuisine, wildlife, and virtual photo spheres.
- **PWA Service Worker v6**: Offline caching expanded for gastronomy trails, wildlife guides, virtual tour viewports, and volunteer listings.
