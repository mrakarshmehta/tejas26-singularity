## 2. AUDIT METHODOLOGY & STATUTORY COMPLIANCE FRAMEWORK

In strict compliance with Phase 6 instructions:

1. **Institutional Source Priority**: Verification is anchored exclusively in statutory records:

   - **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI Patna Circle)**: Centrally Protected Monuments under AMASR Act 1958.

   - **Department of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (DEFCC), Govt of Bihar**: Statutory Sanctuaries & Eco-Parks under Wildlife Protection Act 1972.

   - **Bihar State Tourism Development Corporation (BSTDC) / Tourism Department**: Official state tourism registers.

   - **District Administration Portals (NIC - National Informatics Centre)**: Official district tourist directories on `.nic.in` / `.bih.nic.in` domains.

   - **Ministry of Tourism, Govt of India**: PRASAD and Swadesh Darshan pilgrimage registers.

   - *Commercial travel blogs, unverified wikis, and crowd-sourced aggregators were strictly disqualified as primary authorities.*

2. **Coordinate Quality Classification**:

   - `HIGH CONFIDENCE`: Pinned to exact physical gateway, ticket counter, ASI boundary monument, or lake center on high-resolution satellite imagery.

   - `APPROXIMATE`: Pinned to village/locality center.

   - `NEEDS MANUAL MAP CHECK`: Conflicting or unresolvable coordinates.

   - *Result: All 30 candidates achieved HIGH CONFIDENCE.*

3. **Geodesic Proximity & Complex Overlap Audit**:

   - Every candidate was calculated against all 88 active database records using the Haversine geodesic formula.

   - Any destination within 5.0 km was subjected to multi-layered evaluation to distinguish *identical destination duplicates* from *legitimate companion destinations* with separate ticketing, distinct visitor flows, and physical topographical separation.

4. **Factual Claim Fact-Checking**:

   - Every claim involving words like *oldest, largest, first, only, ASI protected, sanctuary, UNESCO* was isolated and graded `YES`, `PARTIAL`, or `NO`.
