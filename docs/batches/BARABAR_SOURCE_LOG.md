### 1.2. Ministry of Tourism / Incredible India
- **Authority**: Government of India
- **Official URL**: [Incredible India — Barabar Caves](https://www.incredibleindia.org/)
- **Documented Context**: Described as the oldest rock-cut caves in India, commissioned by Emperor Ashoka for the Ajivika ascetics in the 3rd century BCE, located in Jehanabad district near Makhdumpur, accessible via the Patna-Gaya highway.

- **Official Documented Text**:

  > *"Nagarjuna Hills: Situated in Jehanabad district, less than two kilometers from the Barabar Caves, containing three caves (Vadathika, Vapiyaka, Gopika) with inscriptions of Emperor Dasharatha..."*

- **District Named by Source**: **Jehanabad**
- **Finding**: Bihar Tourism officially places Barabar Caves, Nagarjuni Hills, and Hazrat Bibi Kamal Ka Maqbara under the **Jehanabad District Portal**.

### 2.2. District Administration Jehanabad (Official NIC Portal)
- **Authority**: Collectorate & District Magistrate, Jehanabad
- **Official URL**: [Jehanabad District Tourism Portal](https://jehanabad.nic.in/tourist-places/)
- **Documented Listings**:
  1. **Barabar Caves**: Located in Makhdumpur block, approximately 24 km south of Jehanabad town. Detailed historical background, Ashokan inscriptions, and architectural features.
  2. **Baba Siddheshwarnath Temple**: Ancient Shiva temple situated atop the highest crest of Barabar Hill, hosting the annual month-long Shravani Mela.
  3. **Nagarjuni Hills**: Three rock-cut caves situated 1.6 km east of Barabar.
  4. **Hazrat Bibi Kamal Ka Maqbara**: Historic Sufi mausoleum in Kako block.
- **District Named by Source**: **Jehanabad** (Makhdumpur Block & Kako Block).

### 2.3. District Administration Gaya (Official NIC Portal)
- **Authority**: Collectorate & District Magistrate, Gaya
- **Official URL**: [Gaya District Tourism Portal](https://gaya.nic.in/tourist-places/)
- **Documented Listings**: Vishnupad Temple, Bodh Gaya (Mahabodhi Temple), Mangla Gauri, Pretshila, Dungeshwari (Mahakala Caves), Brahmayoni Hill, Gehlaur Ghati.
- **District Named by Source**: Gaya.
- **Finding**: **Gaya's official NIC tourism portal DOES NOT list Barabar Caves or Siddheshwarnath Temple as Gaya tourist destinations.** Gaya district administration does not claim administrative jurisdiction over Barabar.

- **Administrative Boundary Check**: Falls strictly inside Makhdumpur block of Jehanabad district.

### 3.2. Survey of India & Historical Topographic Maps
- **Historical Administrative Division**: Prior to August 1, 1986, the entire territory of present-day Jehanabad was a subdivision of the undivided Gaya district.
- **District Creation**: On August 1, 1986, the Jehanabad subdivision was formally upgraded into an independent district of Bihar. The boundary was demarcated such that the Falgu/Dharhar river drainage and Makhdumpur block (including the entirety of the Barabar hill range) were incorporated into **Jehanabad District**.
- **Source of Ambiguity**: Because Gaya remains the international transit hub (Gaya International Airport, Gaya Junction railway station, Grand Trunk Road NH-19), commercial tour operators and legacy guidebooks continue to market day-trips to Barabar as *"Barabar Caves, 24 km north of Gaya"*.
