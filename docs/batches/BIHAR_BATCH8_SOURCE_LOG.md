## Candidate 1: Ara House
- **District:** Bhojpur (District ID: 16)
- **Category:** `historical`
- **Coordinates:** 25.5539° N, 84.6680° E
- **Canonical Slug:** `ara-house-bhojpur`
- **Evidence Confidence:** **HIGH (Grade A)**

### Primary Authoritative Source
- **Publisher:** Government of Bihar (`bihar.gov.in`)
- **Document Title:** Heritage Sites of Bihar — Ara House, Arrah
- **Direct Portal Listing:** `https://bihar.gov.in` (Tourism & Heritage section)
- **Official Institutional Authority:** Government of Bihar / Directorate of Archaeology & Museums
- **Key Evidence Extracts:**
  > *"Ara House is a historic structure situated in the campus of Maharaja College, south-east of the Ramna Maidan in Arrah town. Originally built prior to 1857 by railway engineer Richard Vicars Boyle, the double-storeyed brick building on a high plinth became the focal point of the famous Siege of Arrah during the 1857 rebellion. A small British garrison and loyal Indian troops held out here for eight days against the massive rebel army led by Babu Veer Kunwar Singh until relieved by Major Vincent Eyre. Today it stands as an officially protected state heritage monument open to visitors."*

### Secondary Supporting Source
- **Publisher:** District Administration Bhojpur (`bhojpur.nic.in`)
- **Document Title:** Places of Historical Interest — Babu Veer Kunwar Singh Freedom Trail
- **Confirmation:** Confirms public visitation (06:00 to 18:00), location inside Maharaja College campus, and historical identity separate from Aranya Devi temple.
