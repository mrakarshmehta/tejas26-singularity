**Project**: HiddenYatra (`D:\HiddenYatra`)

## 4. GOOGLE MAPS & SEARCH ENGINE CROSS-VERIFICATION

For every potential destination discovered through general web and map queries:
1. **Name Normalization**: Cross-checked English, Hindi, and colloquial Maithili/Bhojpuri/Magahi local variants (e.g. *Kabar Taal* vs *Kanwar Lake*; *Mandar Parvat* vs *Mandar Hill*; *Bakarour* vs *Sujata Stupa*).
2. **Geographical Coordinates Verification**: Latitude and longitude verified against official Survey of India grid references and verified geographic pinpoints.
3. **Filter Out Commercial & Non-Tourism Pins**: Filtered out roadside dhabas, generic hotels, coaching institutes, schools, civil courts, administrative offices, and residential parks falsely tagged on Google Maps as "tourist places".
4. **Boundary Verification**: Corrected historical cataloguing anomalies in existing databases (e.g. *Mandar Hill* physically located in Banka district; *Barabar Caves* administratively located in Jehanabad district).

- Every single verified candidate is tethered to an authoritative primary source (State Tourism, District Administration, ASI, or Forest Department).
