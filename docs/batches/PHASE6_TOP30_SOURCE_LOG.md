## 1. INSTITUTIONAL SOURCE HIERARCHY & AUTHORITATIVE REGISTRIES

Every candidate in the Phase 5/6 Top-30 portfolio has been audited exclusively against recognized statutory and governmental authorities in accordance with the Phase 6 directives. Random travel blogs and unverified crowdsourced directories have been strictly excluded from primary verification.


### A. National Statutory Authorities

1. **Archaeological Survey of India (ASI Patna Circle)**:  

   - Statutory custodian of Centrally Protected Monuments under the *Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains Act, 1958 (AMASR Act)*.  

   - Primary Portal: `https://asipatnacircle.bih.nic.in/` | Central ASI: `https://asi.nic.in/`

2. **Ministry of Tourism, Government of India**:  

   - Custodian of the National Pilgrimage Rejuvenation and Spiritual Heritage Augmentation Drive (*PRASAD*) and Swadesh Darshan circuits.  

   - Official Portal: `https://tourism.gov.in/` | `https://www.incredibleindia.org/`

3. **National Tiger Conservation Authority (NTCA) & Wildlife Institute of India (WII)**:  

   - National database of protected areas, wildlife sanctuaries, and proposed tiger reserves.  

   - Official Portal: `https://wii.gov.in/` | `https://ntca.gov.in/`


### B. State Government Statutory Authorities

1. **Department of Tourism, Government of Bihar (BSTDC)**:  

   - Primary state tourism promotional and destination development authority.  

   - Official Portal: `https://tourism.bihar.gov.in/`

2. **Department of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (DEFCC), Bihar**:  

   - Statutory administrator of National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, wetlands, and designated Eco-Tourism Parks.  

   - Official Portal: `https://forest.bihar.gov.in/`

3. **Directorate of Archaeology, Department of Art, Culture & Youth, Govt of Bihar**:  

   - Administrator of State Protected Monuments under the *Bihar Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites, Remains and Art Treasures Act, 1976*.  

   - Official Portal: `https://yac.bihar.gov.in/`

## 3. STATUTORY ACTS & MONUMENT CODES MAPPING


1. **Bateshwar Sthan & Patharghata Caves**: Centrally Protected Monument under ASI Patna Circle (Caves with carvings on Patharghata Hill).

2. **Areraj Someshwar Nath Pillar**: ASI Patna Circle Monument #10 (Lauriya Areraj Edict Pillar of Ashoka, 242 BCE).

3. **Chirand Archaeological Mound**: State Protected Site under Bihar Archaeology Directorate; excavated under Prof. B.P. Sinha.

4. **Rampurva Ashokan Pillars**: ASI Patna Circle Monument #88 (Rampurva Bull & Lion pillar base mounds).

5. **Lauriya Nandangarh**: ASI Patna Circle Monument #89 (Nandangarh 80-ft terraced Stupa & Lauriya Lion Pillar).

6. **Kahalgaon Rock-Cut Sculptures**: ASI Patna Circle Monument #14 (Rock sculptures in the river Ganga at Kahalgaon).

7. **Ashokan Pillar & Ananda Stupa, Kolhua**: ASI Patna Circle Monument #99 (Intact Lion Pillar & Stupa of Ananda at Kolhua).

8. **Buddha Relic Stupa, Vaishali**: ASI Centrally Protected Monument (Stupa 1 / Altekar Excavation 1958).

9. **Kusheshwar Asthan Wildlife Sanctuary**: Wildlife Protection Act (1972) Sanctuary Notification (1994, Bihar Forest Dept).

10. **Kaimur Wildlife Sanctuary**: Wildlife Protection Act Notification (1979, Bihar Forest Dept; NTCA In-Principle Approval 2023).

11. **Udaipur Wildlife Sanctuary**: Wildlife Protection Act Notification (1978, Bihar Forest Dept, 8.87 km² oxbow lake).

12. **Daud Khan Fort**: State Protected Monument under Bihar Ancient Monuments Act, 1976.

13. **Punaura Dham**: National PRASAD Scheme (Ministry of Tourism, Govt of India, 2022 Corridor Sanction).
